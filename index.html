📊 How the "Commissioner Google Sheet" Syncs with Yahoo

Everything on this dashboard updates automatically from the Yahoo Fantasy API each morning, except for three commissioner-only items that Yahoo doesn't track. You can manage those in a simple Google Sheet with 3 tabs:

  • Tab 1 (Owner_Mapping): Columns Season | Yahoo_Team_Key | Owner_Name so historical teams always roll up to the right human owner.
  • Tab 2 (Keeper_Overrides): Columns Player_Name | Current_Owner | Locked (TRUE/FALSE) | Custom_Keeper_Round to lock in final 3 keepers per team or resolve same-round conflicts.
  • Tab 3 (Trade_Picks): Columns Trade_Date | Team_A | Team_A_Picks_Received | Team_B | Team_B_Picks_Received to display future draft picks alongside Yahoo's player trade log.
